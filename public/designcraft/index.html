Included now

  • Recovered PDFs and original OTE logo artwork.
  • 104 reconstructed recipes, PDF catalogue and native examples.
  • Issue content/media ZIP export in OTEManager.
  • Bundled official DesignCraft web editor, with direct document opening and browser saves/exports.
  • Optional server composition for editable native, PDF/X-4 and IDML output.
  • Native cover and centre-spread preservation checks.
  • Shared custom-block fields and saved boundaries.

Fonts & branding

Original issues show their existing branding. Editable examples use Source Sans 3 and Source Serif 4, included under their open font licences.

The 2025 covers reference Decalotype Black, Helvetica Neue and Salted. Salted Regular is bundled and loads automatically in the editor, with its supplied personal-use-only notice. Helvetica fonts remain privately configured for server composition. The OTE 2024–25 and 2025–26 house designs are recreated from the original InDesign files; without the licensed fonts they use open look-alikes (Barlow, TeX Gyre Heros, Caveat Brush). Fonts you choose in the web editor or the browser composer stay on your device.

Remaining work

  • Resolve source font substitutions, missing links and original text overflow.
  • Configure automatic composition on the production OTE server.
  • Test generated IDML in Adobe InDesign.
  • Print a proof of the original-design pages and compare them with a printed issue.
  • Keep editor changes and saved planner recipes coordinated through their source records.

Keep the work with the project.

The review pages, PDFs, recipes and compiler source are tracked in OTEManager. The complete ZIP also includes byte-identical recovered originals.
