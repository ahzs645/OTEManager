# Pedestrian Safety

Prince George RCMP is urging both drivers and pedestrians to exercise extra caution following two pedestrian-involved accidents within 24 hours this January. Speaking with CFUR Radio, Media Relations Officer Corporal Jennifer Cooper emphasized that winter’s extended dark hours and black winter clothing create heightened risks.

“Road safety is everyone’s responsibility,” Cooper shared from UNBC’s campus. She recommends drivers take extra time to check intersections and avoid distractions, while pedestrians should make eye contact with drivers and consider wearing reflective gear.

The timing isn’t unusual, as December and January typically see an increase in pedestrian-vehicle incidents. Cooper suggests simple solutions like removing earbuds and ensuring vehicle headlights are on during twilight hours.
