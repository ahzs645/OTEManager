# Success Has No Single Map: Why Trying Something New Could Be Your Breakthrough

Byline: Florence Ejodame Oisereime

People often say the secret is simple: attend job fairs, turn up at networking events, hang out in the right spaces, and you will meet the right people.

And yes, sometimes that works. Opportunities can appear when you walk into a room full of strangers or exchange a few words with someone at an industry mixer. But here is the twist: success does not follow just one map.

For someone who has never stepped into a networking room, maybe that is the new path to try. For another person, it could mean starting a blog, reaching out to a mentor online, or volunteering somewhere they have never considered before. What opens doors for one individual might not work for another, and that is perfectly fine.

The truth is, there is no single road to success. We find our way by being open, experimenting, and daring to step into the unfamiliar. Growth often lives outside our comfort zone.

So today, challenge yourself: try something new. It might feel uncertain at first, but you never know where that bold step might lead. Your breakthrough could be waiting in the very place you have yet to explore.
