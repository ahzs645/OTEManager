# Inside the Edge

**Question**

I’ve noticed some articles in Over the Edge seem like they could be AI-generated. Can you clarify your stance on AI usage and explain how you ensure the content is original and student-driven?

**Answer**

Thanks for asking about AI in Over the Edge! Yes, we do use AI tools, and we want to be upfront about that. While our students are the creative force behind every story, we use AI to help with things like smoothing out paragraph transitions, grammar checks, and making our writing flow more naturally. Right now, we’re experimenting with these tools to figure out what works best - it’s very much a learning process for all of us. Everything you read still comes from our student journalists’ minds and experiences, with AI just helping to polish the final product. We’re learning and improving as we go, and we really appreciate feedback from our readers as we work to find the right balance. Student journalism is still at the heart of everything we do!
