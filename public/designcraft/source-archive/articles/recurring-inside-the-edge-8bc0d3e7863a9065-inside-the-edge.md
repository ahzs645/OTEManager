# Inside the Edge

**Question**

Are there any topics that are off-limits? How does OTE handle controversial or sensitive subjects?

**Answer**

At Over the Edge, we’re open to all topics you’re passionate about writing on! While no subjects are off-limits, we do consider where pieces might fit best. Our print newspaper, which goes out through our media partnership, typically features content that speaks to a broader community audience. But don’t worry if your piece tackles more controversial or mature themes – our website is a perfect platform for these stories, giving writers more flexibility to explore complex or sensitive topics. Our editorial team is always here to chat about the best home for your work and help you shape your ideas.
